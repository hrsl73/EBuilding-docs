1. Where Jobs Run

  • Every deployed environment (production, staging, dev server) starts all jobs. A developer's machine (NODE_ENV=local) starts only the maintenance bill reminder.
  • Database export is the one job whose time depends on the environment: daily at 04:30 on production, monthly on the 1st at 00:00 elsewhere.
  • Backfill maintenance runs every 30 minutes but does nothing unless NODE_ENV is production or development and BACKFILL_MAINTENANCE_CRON_ENABLED isn't false.
  • Jobs are created stopped in cron-jobs.js and started in server.js.

26 jobs in total: 17 daily, 8 recurring through the day, 2 monthly (the database export counts once as daily on production and once as monthly elsewhere).

6. Free Slots

Hours with no daily or monthly job, for picking a time for a new one. Avoid minutes :00, :10, :15, :30 and :45 in any hour, where the recurring jobs fire.

Window (IST)Before / after
05:00–06:59After the production database export (04:30); before the subscription cron (07:00).
09:00–09:59After the 08:00 reminders; before personal reminders (10:00). Daytime traffic.
11:00–23:58No daily job until the 23:59 place booking expiry. Daytime and evening traffic.
💰
For the Finance monthly collection entry
It runs once on the 1st for the month that just ended. Decided time: 05:20 IST (inside the 05:00–06:59 window, after the database export, off the busy minute marks). See the BRD.
